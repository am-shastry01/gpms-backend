Add a company

This creates the company, its warehouses and the admin account that runs them. The admin signs in to the app with the email below and is asked to choose their own password straight away.

Company

Goes in front of every number this company issues — YROBE-GP-2026-000001. Letters and digits, 2 to 12.

Address

This address prints in the box at the foot of every gate pass the company issues, with its phone, email and website.

Warehouses
Warehouse name * Number

The number is yours to choose — type it how you want it, like indorewh1. Rows left blank are ignored, and a row with no number gets WH-01, WH-02 in order. Numbers only have to be unique inside this company, so two clients can both have a WH-01.

Their admin

The email is how they sign in — no username to invent, and two companies can both employ a Ravi. The password is temporary: the app makes them set their own on first sign-in, after which you no longer know it.
